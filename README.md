# Hi, I'm Cristy 👋

### Microbiologist & Bioinformatician · PhD in Biotechnology

I work where the **lab bench meets the terminal**. Trained as a microbiologist and with a PhD in biotechnology working with microorganisms, I turn complex sequencing data into reliable, reproducible biological insight — from DNA in a tube to the figure that answers the question. My core is **microbiome and metagenomics** (16S and shotgun): taxonomic and functional profiling, community ecology, and biomarker discovery.

### 🔬 Scientist side — microbiology & biotechnology
- Microbial ecology & community analysis; cultivation and characterisation of microorganisms
- Biotechnology with microorganisms: bioremediation, metallotolerance, greenhouse-gas mitigation, fungal melanins
- Wet-lab: DNA/RNA extraction, NGS sample context, FISH, quality control under GMP/ISO
- Experimental design and statistics for biological data
